Who We Are

About TouchBase Foundation

TouchBase Foundation for Social and Sports Development is a registered Non-Profit Company (NPC) dedicated to uplifting communities from Komani (Queenstown) and beyond.

We ensure the wellbeing and support of our members while facilitating the identification of communities' and individuals' social and sporting needs. We mobilise resources, provide platforms for community outreach, and act as a gateway between talent and institutions of learning or support.

Our Aim

To contribute to the upliftment of communities from disadvantaging and disempowering conditions, and to ensure the wellbeing and support of the members of the organisation.

Our Approach

We identify needs, mobilise resources, form partnerships with individuals and organisations, and provide meaningful platforms for launching community outreach and sporting programmes.

Our Values

We act with good faith and without malice towards each other. We offer advice and guidance in matters of personal and professional significance.

Our Purpose

Primary Objectives

01

Identify Needs

Facilitate the identification of communities' and individuals' social and sporting needs.

02

Mobilise Resources

Assemble resources to address the identified needs — funding, equipment, facilities, and services.

03

Launch Programmes

Provide a meaningful and resource-worthy platform for launching and supporting community outreach and sporting programmes.

04

Support Talent

Identify, support, and enhance talent in our communities.

05

Gateway to Opportunity

Become a gateway between talent and institutions of learning or institutions of support.

06

Member Wellbeing

Provide support to each member — organising events, functions, and ensuring the wellbeing of all.

What We Do

Our Programmes

We run programmes that address real community needs — from sports development to social support.

Sports Development

Providing sporting equipment, uniforms, facilities access, and structured programmes to develop athletic talent in underserved communities.

Talent & Education Gateway

Connecting talented individuals with institutions of learning, bursaries, and mentorship opportunities to unlock their potential.

Community Outreach

Addressing social needs through clothing drives, health services and equipment, transportation support, accommodation assistance, and community events.

Member Support

Bereavement contributions, congratulatory gifts for milestones (marriage, birth, new home), and hosting of TouchBase sessions and events.

Health & Wellness

Health services, related equipment, and wellness programmes to support the physical wellbeing of community members.

TouchBase Events & Trips

Organising foundation events, functions, and trips that strengthen bonds and create shared experiences among members.

Structure

Our Governance

Transparent, accountable, and structured for impact.

Board of Directors

Members who constitute the foundation's membership with voting rights and powers to appoint executive management.

Executive Management

5 officers elected annually to oversee day-to-day operations with dignity, due diligence, and respect.

Finance Committee

Ensures soundness of finances, assists with collection and allocation of funds, and issues donation certificates.

Organising Committee

Coordinates, organises, and mobilises sources of funding and manages programme delivery.

Governance Committee

Responsible for governance, structure, direction, discipline, and constitutional adherence.

Annual General Meeting

Held annually to elect management, hear financial statements, and consider all foundation matters.
